      How we work

      Four steps, in English or Spanish, with a real person from the team on WhatsApp.

      1. 1

        We talk

        You tell us how your business works and what you want to solve. We send you a proposal with scope, timeline and a fixed price in USD.

      2. 2

        We design

        We show you how it will look and work before we write any code, so you can adjust whatever you need.

      3. 3

        We build

        We build in stages and share progress you can try yourself.

      4. 4

        We launch and support

        We publish it, show you how to use it and stay available for changes and improvements.
